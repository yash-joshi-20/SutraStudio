## 1. Structured Keyword Taxonomies

The platform enforces contextual keyword selection. Rather than blindly appending buzzwords, prompts query keywords filtered by provider capability and scene context.

### 1. Cinematic Keywords
- `cinematic lighting` — Soft 3200K multi-point key and rim lighting.
- `establishing shot` — Wide architectural framing grounding spatial scale.
- `drone reveal` — Ascending flight path cresting over barriers.
- `aerial orbit` — Continuous 360-degree curved orbit maintaining focal lock.
- `smooth dolly-in` — Gradual physical forward movement creating intimacy.
- `tracking shot` — Lateral camera gliding parallel to architectural surfaces.
- `macro detail` — Extreme close-up of paper grain, brass seals, and stone finishes.
- `shallow depth of field` — Crisp subject focus with f/1.8 creamy bokeh.
- `parallax` — Dynamic separation between foreground pillars and background atriums.
- `volumetric lighting` — God rays filtering through jali lattice screens.
- `architectural fly-through` — Continuous glide from exterior entry into interior studio.
- `visual continuity` — Rigid preservation of light temperature and geography across shots.

### 2. Educational Reel Keywords
- `scroll-stopping hook` — High-contrast headline within initial 1.5 seconds.
- `step-by-step tutorial` — 3-part progression breaking complex workflows into bite-sized steps.
- `screen recording` — Authentic lossless capture of Next.js / Figma environments.
- `cursor highlight` — Soft golden radial beacon (#A98B5733) with click wave.
- `zoom callout` — 1.4x smooth bezier crop onto interactive buttons.
- `comparison layout` — Side-by-side Before vs After visual split.
- `animated captions` — Word-by-word synchronized subtitles with active word highlight.
- `kinetic typography` — Dynamic text scaling and spring overshoot.
- `progress indicator` — 2px gold timeline bar running along screen border.
- `CTA` — Unambiguous closing instruction directing viewer to bio link.

### 3. Motion Graphics Keywords
- `kinetic typography`, `animated infographic`, `number counter`, `shape transition`, `lower third`, `smooth easing`.

### 4. Interior & 3D Keywords
- `photorealistic interior`, `material visualization`, `3D product turntable`, `equirectangular panorama`, `360 virtual tour`, `daylight simulation`.

### 5. Marketing Keywords
- `brand consistency`, `value proposition`, `audience pain point`, `benefit-led messaging`, `social proof`.
