## 1. Overview & Categorization

The prompt library contains production-grade, versioned prompt templates classified into five primary disciplines:

### Category A: Trending Educational Reels
- `prompt-edu-ai-tools-explained` (v2.1.0) — High-impact 9:16 vertical explainer with kinetic subtitles and cursor highlights.
- `prompt-edu-mistakes-vs-best-practices` (v1.4.0) — Split-screen two-column comparison highlighting client pitfalls vs studio best practices.

### Category B: Screen Recording & UI Demonstrations
- `prompt-screen-dashboard-walkthrough` (v2.0.0) — Lossless desktop walkthrough with camera zoom (1.3x) onto primary CTA buttons and output badges.

### Category C: Marketing & Advertising
- `prompt-mktg-brand-introduction` (v3.0.0) — Luxury brand commercial blending natural materials (teak, limestone) with modern digital interfaces.

### Category D: Cinematic Video
- `prompt-cinematic-drone-architectural` (v2.2.0) — Ultra-photorealistic 16:9 aerial orbit with volumetric daylight god rays and natural depth of field.

### Category E: 3D, Interior and 360°
- `prompt-interior-3d-transformation` (v2.0.0) — Dynamic interior room transformation wipe, material texturing, and 360° equirectangular panoramic capture.

## 2. Standard Template Anatomy

Each template contains:
1. **Template ID & Semantic Version**
2. **Category & Intended Audience**
3. **Required Variable Inputs** (`{{tool_name}}`, `{{industry}}`, `{{common_mistake}}`)
4. **Prompt Template**
5. **Shot & Scene Progression Breakdown**
6. **Strict Negative Constraints** (No neon glow, no fake statistics, no low-res artifacts)
7. **Compatible AI Capabilities**
8. **Output Specifications** (Aspect ratio, FPS, resolution)
9. **Estimated Compute Cost**
10. **Fallback Degradation Path**
