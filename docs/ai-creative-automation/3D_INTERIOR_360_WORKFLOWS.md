## 1. Spatial Technology Standards

1. **Equirectangular Panoramic Projection:**
   - 2:1 aspect ratio (4096x2048) panoramic images formatted for seamless spherical projection.
   - Zero pinching or warping artifacts at the zenith (top) and nadir (bottom) poles.
2. **Interactive Three.js WebGL Viewer:**
   - Client-side WebGL canvas rendering smooth orbit controls, field-of-view adjustments, and clickable hotspot pins.
   - Lightweight initial bundle with progressive texture loading.
3. **PBR Material Specifications:**
   - Accurate roughness, metallic, normal, and ambient occlusion maps reflecting natural teak wood, travertine stone, and unlacquered brass.
4. **Daylight Cycle Simulations:**
   - Time-lapse keyframe rendering simulating 5500K noon sunlight transitioning to 2800K dusk.
