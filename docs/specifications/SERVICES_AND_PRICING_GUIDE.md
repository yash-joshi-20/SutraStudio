## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

**Sutra Studio** delivers creative technology and architectural visualization through two commercial models:
1. **Standalone / Individual Disciplines (Multi-Select with Quantity Counter)**: For specific, on-demand projects where clients purchase discrete quantities of deliverables.
2. **Monthly Retainer Plans (3-Day Free Trial Included)**: For continuous creative capacity, dedicated art direction, high-volume production, priority SLAs, and bundled savings.

---

## 2. How the Quantity Multiplier (1, 2, 3...) Works

In the Client Dashboard (`/orders` -> *Individual Services*):
* **Quantity = 1**: Represents **1 Complete Production Unit / Package** for that discipline (e.g. 1 Image Creation package = 3 to 5 multi-angle 4K renders for 1 product/concept; 1 Video Creation = 1 cinematic commercial ad in dual aspect ratios).
* **Quantity = 2, 3, etc.**: Directly scales the volume of assets and products linearly:
  * **2 × Image Creation (₹10,998)** = 2 distinct products/concepts (6–10 total 4K master renders).
  * **3 × 3D Modeling (₹28,497)** = 3 distinct CAD/product 3D models with full PBR textures and WebGL exports.
* **Live Dynamic Total**: `Subtotal = Unit Starting Price × Quantity`.
* **Single Brief or Multi-Asset Upload**: Clients provide details for each unit in the dynamic brief or attach a bulk ZIP/Drive link containing references for all units.

## 4. Monthly Retainer Plans: How Fulfillment & Daily Operations Work

### Common Client Question: *"If I pay monthly, do I receive assets daily, and how does the studio fulfill requests?"*

#### **How Fulfillment Operates**:
1. **Continuous Queue / Sprint Workflow**:
   - Monthly Retainer is **not** a rigid "1 post sent at 9 AM daily" setup; instead, it is an **always-on creative pipeline with dedicated capacity**.
   - As soon as the client submits a request or brief in their dashboard, the studio works in 24–48 hour turnaround sprints.
   - Deliverables flow continuously throughout the month until the monthly quota is fulfilled.
2. **Batch & Scheduled Delivery Option**:
   - If the client prefers all monthly social media/video creatives delivered at once for scheduling, the studio prepares a complete **Monthly Drop** within the first 5–7 days of the billing cycle.
3. **3-Day Free Trial Protection**:
   - When a client activates any Retainer Plan, their card is staged with a **3-Day Free Trial**.
   - The studio kicks off initial sample renders/brief onboarding immediately. If the client cancels within 3 days, ₹0 is charged.
